FAQ

What you
ask us

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll reply straight away.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need an appointment?

We recommend booking, especially for colour and skincare. If there’s a free slot we’ll see you straight away — just message us on WhatsApp.

How long does a balayage take?

Between two and three hours, depending on length and starting shade. We’ll give you the exact time and price at your consultation.

Can I change or cancel my appointment?

Of course. Let us know 24 hours in advance and there’s no charge.

Do you do bridal hair on location?

Yes, in and around Girona. We include a trial at the salon so everything is perfect on the day.

Which products do you use?

Vegan, ammonia-free colour and natural skincare for treatments. If you have any allergies, let us know when you book.

How can I pay?

By card, cash or Bizum.
